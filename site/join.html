Become a LADS Member

Membership is $10 per academic year.

Open to all dental students at BAU, LU, and USJ. Membership runs per academic year.

ExchangesAccess international exchange programs through IADS.
WorkshopsPriority access and member rates for all LADS events.
TrainingSoft-skill and professional development sessions.
CommunityA network of dental students across all three universities.

Registration opens in September

Online registration and whish transfers will go live in September, at the start of the academic year. The form below is not accepting submissions yet.

Until then you can still join. Contact us on Instagram, reach us through our WhatsApp channels, or speak to your university delegate.

Already a member? Create your account

The member portal shows your dues and renewal date, lets you register for events, and lets you apply for exchanges and voluntary projects. Use the same email address you registered with and your membership links up on its own.

How to Join

Three steps, about five minutes.

Pay your dues through whish

Membership is $10 USD per academic year. From September, send payment via the whish app to the number below and take a screenshot of the confirmation.

Fill the registration form

Complete the form below with your details and upload your payment screenshot.

Get confirmed

Our Treasurer verifies your payment and you receive your membership confirmation by email.
