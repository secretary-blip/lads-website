Our Impact

What LADS delivers, in practice.

LADS is a volunteer-run non-profit. Every program below is delivered by dental students without payment, and is funded by member dues of 10 USD per academic year together with sponsorship secured by our Fundraising Committee.

Our charitable work falls into three areas: free oral health care for underserved communities, oral health education in schools and public settings, and professional development for dental students across Lebanon's three dental faculties.

Committee heads report to the Executive Committee, which reports to the membership at General Assemblies.

Where to verify our work

Our Instagram account is the primary and most complete public record of LADS activity. Every campaign, clinic day, school visit, workshop and general assembly is documented there as it happens, with photographs, dates and locations. It is updated continuously by our Social Media Committee and is the best place to see and verify what we actually do.

We also maintain a Facebook page and a LinkedIn page. This website is being expanded to carry a fuller archive of our events.

Free care

Pain relief and basic dental treatment delivered at no cost to people who cannot access or afford dental care.

Prevention & education

School visits, screenings and public campaigns teaching oral hygiene and early detection to children and adults.

Student development

Research, training, and international exchange opportunities for dental students at BAU, LU and USJ.
