Announcements from the Board.

2026

LADS signs MOU with the Lebanese Pharmacy Students Association (LPSA)

LADS and LPSA formalized their partnership through a Memorandum of Understanding, opening the door to joint events, shared training opportunities, and inter-professional collaboration between dental and pharmacy students in Lebanon.

2026

New LADS Board takes office

A new fifteen-member Board was formed, comprising the Executive Committee and the heads of all nine standing committees, with a mandate focused on strengthening LADS structures and deepening collaboration with IADS.

2026

Membership registration now open

Dental students at BAU, LU, and USJ can now register online and pay their annual dues at any LADS event. Membership gives access to exchanges, workshops, training, and all LADS events.
