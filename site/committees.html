Our Committees

Nine standing committees drive everything LADS does.

Every member can get involved.

01

Scientific Committee

Encourages dental students to engage in medical and scientific research with local professionals and international initiatives. Organizes conferences, lectures, and workshops. Works with the IADS SCORE program.

Head: Tia Majed
02

Fundraising Committee

Secures sponsorships and financial support, communicates with companies and partners, and prepares sponsorship agreements ensuring the Association's financial sustainability.

Heads: Alaa Al Kork & Kristina Hajj Geagea
03

Exchange Committee

Organizes the annual exchange period and international exchange opportunities for members, led by the National Exchange Officer in coordination with the IADS Exchange Board.

NEO: Ali Khalifeh
04

Training Committee

Runs training sessions during General Assemblies and beyond, offering members professional and soft-skill development in accordance with the IADS Training Committee.

Head: Aya Omais
05

Public Health Committee

Raises awareness about common diseases and destructive habits among students and local communities. Promotes healthy habits through campaigns, screening, and treatment initiatives.

Head: Maysa Abou Saad
06

Voluntary Committee

Provides opportunities to contribute to oral health care in underprivileged regions, evaluates volunteering opportunities, and serves as Local Organizing Committee for IADS International Voluntary Projects.

Head: Fouad Haytham Mawlawy
07

Activities Committee

Organizes trips, gatherings, sports days, and clubs for theater, music, photography, and more, helping students enjoy life beyond their studies.

Head: Majida Jabr
08

Editorial Committee

Produces the visual content representing LADS: the LADS Gazette, posters, promotional graphics, and the Association's visual identity, in accordance with the IADS Editorial Board.

Head: Aya Al Ghandour
09

Social Media Committee

Manages all LADS social platforms, produces engaging content, runs digital campaigns, and builds our online community while promoting dental health awareness.

Head: Sarah Sadikk
