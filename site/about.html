About LADS

Who we are and what we stand for.

Our Association

Registered, student-run, and represented internationally.

The Lebanese Association of Dental Students (LADS) is a non-profit, non-political NGO founded in 2015 in Beirut. We represent the interests of dental students across Lebanon and connect them with opportunities in research, training, exchange, public health, and community service.

Our members come from the three dental faculties of Lebanon: Beirut Arab University (BAU), the Lebanese University (LU), and Université Saint-Joseph (USJ). LADS is affiliated with the International Association of Dental Students (IADS), giving members direct access to international congresses, exchange programs, research networks, and training opportunities.

LADS is officially registered with the Lebanese government and operates under its constitution and internal bylaws. The Board consists of fifteen members: the Executive Committee and the Head of each standing committee.

Governance

How the Association is run.

LADS operates under a written constitution and internal bylaws. The Association is run entirely by dental students; there are no paid staff and no political affiliation.

The Board consists of fifteen members: a five-person Executive Committee (President, Vice-President, General Secretary, Treasurer, and Delegate to IADS) and the Head of each of the nine standing committees. Board positions are held for a one-year mandate.

Membership dues are set for each academic year and published on the membership page. Dues are collected and verified by the Treasurer, who is a member of the Executive Committee, and are used solely to fund the Association's programs and activities.

Our beneficiaries

LADS serves two groups: dental students at the three dental faculties in Lebanon (Beirut Arab University, the Lebanese University, and Université Saint-Joseph), and underserved communities across Lebanon who receive free oral health care and education through our voluntary and public health programs.
