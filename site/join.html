How to Join

Two things, in this order.

Registering and creating a portal account are separate steps, and they have to happen in that order. Register first so the Treasurer has your payment; make your account afterwards to see it confirmed.

1

Pay your dues through whish

Membership is $10 USD per academic year. From September, send payment through the whish app to the number in the panel, and screenshot the confirmation.

2

Fill in the form below

Your details, your payment screenshot, and a few questions about what you would actually like to do with LADS.

3

Create your portal account

Use the same email address. Your membership links up on its own, and you can then see your dues, register for events and apply for projects.

Do not create your account first

An account created before you register will simply show Not paid until the Treasurer confirms your payment. Nothing is lost if you do it the other way round, but registering first means it is right immediately.
