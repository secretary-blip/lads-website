Become a LADS Member

Membership runs
for the academic year.

Registration opens in September

whish transfers go live in September, at the start of the academic year. You can create your account now, and pay as soon as it opens.

Questions before then? Reach us on Instagram, through our WhatsApp channels, or speak to your university delegate.

How it works

Three steps.

1

Create your account

Name, email, university, year. Takes a minute, and it is free. You do not need to have paid anything to have an account.

2

Pay your dues

One payment for the whole academic year, through whish. The amount is shown when you pay. Upload the screenshot from inside the portal.

3

Get confirmed

Our Treasurer verifies the transfer. Your account updates the moment it is confirmed, and you get an email either way.
